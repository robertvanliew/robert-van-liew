How much does a musician website cost?

2026 Prices DIY vs Custom DJs Bands EPKs
Short answer: a musician or DJ website costs anywhere from about $8 a month on a DIY music builder to $1,500 to $8,000 for a custom site from a freelancer, and more from an agency. Where you land depends on one question: does the site just need to exist, or does it need to win bookings from people who've never heard of you? This guide breaks down the real 2026 numbers, what drives them, and when DIY is the smarter call. I'm a designer and developer who builds music websites, so I've included my own pricing at the end, but the rest of the numbers come from public pricing pages and guides.

What Drives the Price

Why One Site Costs $500 and Another $5,000

The biggest cost driver isn't the number of pages. It's how much the site has to do on its own.

Design
Template vs custom
A template is cheap because someone else already designed it. Custom costs more and looks like you.

Selling
Store or tickets

Features

Players, games, or anything built from scratch is the biggest jump in cost.
Moving
Platform migration
Moving off another builder means carrying content over and redirecting old URLs.
Search
SEO and structured data
Setting up how Google and AI tools understand you is often skipped on cheap builds.
Do You Need a Custom Site?

When DIY Is Enough, and When It Isn't

DIY is usually enough if you mostly play locally, your bookings come through people who already know you, and you just need somewhere to send them. Some booking agents say a link page or a Bandcamp profile can be enough for acts that only play locally (Loud and Heavy Booking). Spend the money on recording or photos instead.

A custom site pays off when the website itself has to do the selling: you book weddings, private events, or corporate work where people search Google first and compare options; you send an EPK to bookers and festivals who judge you in seconds; you sell tickets or merch; or you're tired of looking like every other artist on the same template. The simplest test is to compare the price of the site with what one extra booking a year is worth to you.
