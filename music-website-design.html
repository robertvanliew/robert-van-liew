03 · Process

How a Project Runs

01 · Inquiry
Tell me what you do, who books you, and what's not working with your current site. I'll reply with a scope, a price, and a timeline.
02 · Design
I design the look and the booking path around your music and your audience, and we review it together before anything is built.
03 · Build
I build the site, write the structured data, set up your inquiry form, and test it on real phones.
04 · Launch
Your domain goes live, old URLs are redirected if you're moving from another platform, and the site is submitted to Google and Bing.

05 · Questions

Frequently Asked

How much does a musician or DJ website cost?

Projects start at $2,500. A focused site with a bio, music, photos, an EPK section, events, and a booking form usually lands near that starting point. Online stores, ticketing, custom interactive features, or moving a large site off another platform add to it. See the full cost guide for how that compares with DIY builders, freelancers, and agencies.

Do you only work with artists in New York and New Jersey?

No. I'm based in New York and work with a lot of NYC and NJ artists, but I build for musicians, DJs, and event brands anywhere in the US. Everything runs over email and video calls.

Can you move my site off Squarespace, Wix, or Bandzoogle?

Yes. I rebuild the site, carry your content over, and redirect your old page addresses to the new ones so the search visibility you've already built isn't lost. I did exactly this for Bangin' & Beats, redirecting all 46 old URLs.

Will a new website get me more bookings?

No one can honestly promise that. What the site does is make you easy to find and easy to book: it loads fast on a phone, tells Google and AI search tools exactly who you are and where you play, and puts a clear inquiry form in front of anyone ready to hire you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The site and the domain are yours. You're not renting a template from a platform, and you can take it anywhere.

How does payment work?

One payment for the project, made upfront before work starts. No retainers and no monthly fees to me.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. The DMC Canada site launched in a one-week sprint and the Julie Schatz rebuild took two weeks. I'll give you a timeline once I know what you need.

Can you build just an EPK?

Yes. An EPK can be a section of your site or a standalone page you send to bookers and venues, with your bio, photos, music, press, and booking contact in one link.
