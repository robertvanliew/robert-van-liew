Squarespace vs Bandzoogle vs a custom site

2026 Prices Bandzoogle Squarespace Custom Musicians
Short answer: Bandzoogle is the cheapest way to get music tools like a player, merch store, and press kit in one place, at $9.16 to $18.33 a month billed yearly. Squarespace gives you better-looking templates for $19 to $99 a month, but you build the music pieces yourself. A custom site costs more up front and makes sense when the website has to win bookings from people who have never heard of you. Here is how they compare on price, fees, features, and what happens if you want to leave.

Costs People Miss

What the Plan Price Leaves Out

Fees on sales. Card processing applies everywhere, and Squarespace adds a 2% store fee on its cheapest plan. If you sell merch or tickets, compare the plan with the fee, not just the monthly price.

Monthly adds up. Five years on a $25 plan is $1,500, and you still do not own the design.

Switching later. Moving off a builder means rebuilding the site and redirecting every old address so Google does not lose track of you. When I rebuilt Bangin' & Beats, that meant redirecting 46 old URLs one by one.

Your time. A DIY site is cheap in dollars. It is not cheap in evenings, especially the press kit, booking form, and search setup.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Is Bandzoogle or Squarespace better for musicians?

Bandzoogle is better if you want music tools built in, such as a player, merch store, mailing list, and EPK, at a lower price. Squarespace is better if design matters more to you and you are comfortable building the music pieces yourself.

How much does Bandzoogle cost?

As of November 2026, Bandzoogle website plans cost $9.16 to $18.33 a month billed yearly, or $11 to $22 month to month, with a 14-day free trial.

How much does Squarespace cost?

As of November 2026, Squarespace plans cost $19 to $99 a month billed yearly, or $25 to $139 month to month. The Basic plan charges a 2% fee on store sales; higher plans do not.

When is a custom website worth it for a musician?

When the website has to win bookings or sales from people who do not know you yet, such as wedding couples, corporate clients, or bookers comparing options. If one extra booking a year covers the cost, it usually is.

Can I move from Squarespace or Bandzoogle to a custom site later?

Yes. Your content gets carried over and your old page addresses get redirected to the new ones, so the search visibility you have already built is kept.
